## Fidelity boundary from primary FRC research

[Spencer, Tuszewski & Linford (1983)](https://physics.byu.edu/docs/publication/2232) treats coupled FRC compression. [Ma et al. (NIMROD/NIMEQ, 2022), section 2 and table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.08280v1) explicitly includes magnetic energy, equilibrium beta, axial contraction and external magnetic-field compression. Its simulations show density/temperature sensitivity to initial profiles and field ramp rate. Therefore fixed-length `T∝V^(−2/3)` is an imposed gas closure, not a validated FRC scaling. The model documentation makes this distinction; the `β=1` readout is a pressure-equivalent estimate, not a predicted coil field.

The next necessary scientific extension before device prediction is a specified FRC equilibrium/compression closure benchmarked against such an existing calculation. Separate ion/electron energy, reconnection/kinetic losses and an explicitly coupled circuit would then need their own controls. Those are missing physical closures, not evidence that the current conditional ideal-gas arithmetic is wrong. No public matched Laurelin operating point was found in the reviewed pages.
