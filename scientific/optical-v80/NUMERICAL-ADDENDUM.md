# Additional numerical qualification

Independent pre-run review found that centroid mesh convergence alone cannot
qualify the prescribed temperature-gradient spectrum: a full-line centroid does
not depend on thermal width. Retain the original frozen protocol and additionally
compare spectral mode and analytic variance between the last two optical meshes,
at the finest spectral bins, for every temperature/instrument sensitivity.
Require each relative change below 1%; retain every failure. This gate was added
before optical comparison results were generated, not selected by velocity fit.
