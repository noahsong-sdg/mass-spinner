          What this version resolves

          Orbit models calculate full paths, impact energy and residence time with specified source velocities, mass, charge and optional elastic neutral collisions. Centrifuge models use common-corotation, specified-field Gaussian or coupled magnetic Gaussian equilibria, with central or whole-cylinder composition. They calculate current and field diagnostics and cylindrical inventory. Separate-seed studies and B/V/mass scans show sampling uncertainty. The rotating filter includes a separate analytic cold-orbit reference. Orbit chamber radius: 0.48 m · length: 2.8 m · duration: 0.6 ms.

          What it assumes

          Orbit fields and neutral cross sections are inputs. The neutral bath stays cold. Rotating and ICR modes exclude Coulomb collisions. The separate double-well model includes local binary ion–ion scattering. All exclude charge exchange, self-consistent wave coupling, sheaths and turbulence.

          Centrifuge equilibria specify common rigid ion rotation. The specified-field Gaussian model gives a self-field estimate. The coupled magnetic Gaussian model calculates radial B, current and potential. These equilibria exclude electron inertia, driven discharge and instability. They do not calculate transient separation.

          Double-well dynamics use equal-weight particles, specified fields and a small-angle Coulomb operator. Examine numerical torque and resolution before use of transport results. Hardware is for illustration. These models do not give real-device throughput or power.
