          What this version resolves

          Full ion orbits, drifting thermal or fixed-energy sources, configurable mass/charge, optional elastic neutral collisions, impact energy and residence time. The centrifuge offers common-corotation, imposed-field Gaussian and coupled magnetic Gaussian closures, central or whole-cylinder composition, current/field diagnostics and cylindrical inventory integration. Independent-seed studies and B/V/mass scans expose sampling uncertainty. The rotating filter includes an independent analytic cold-orbit reference. Chamber radius 0.48 m · length 2.8 m · 0.6 ms orbit window.

          What it assumes

          Orbit fields are prescribed; the neutral cross section is assumed and the cold bath does not heat up. The rotating/ICR orbit modes omit Coulomb collisions; the separate double-well kinetic model includes local binary ion–ion scattering. Charge exchange, self-consistent wave coupling, sheaths and turbulent transport are omitted. All centrifuge closures prescribe common rigid ion rotation. The imposed-field Gaussian estimates self-field; the coupled magnetic Gaussian solves B, current and potential radially. Electron inertia, driven discharge and instability remain omitted. None is a transient separator. Double-well dynamics use equal-weight particles, prescribed fields and a small-angle Coulomb operator; numerical torque and resolution diagnostics must be checked before interpreting transport. Hardware is conceptual; no real-device throughput or power is predicted.
