How Mass Spinner works

Electrical drive → gas rotation → mass separation.

What moves, and why

Current turns ions; collisions turn neutrals. Radial transport changes composition and inertia; optional thermal evolution tracks heat. The vessel stays still.

dp/dr = ρΩ²r
ns(r) ∝ exp[msΩ²r²/(2kBT)]

These equilibrium references assume ideal, isothermal, rigidly rotating neutral gas. Heavier atoms favor the wall more strongly. Faster rotation helps sorting; heating opposes it. The live model does not solve radial fluid momentum or extraction.

Torque and heat

dLi/dt = τem − Γ(Ωi − Ωn)
dLn/dt = Γ(Ωi − Ωn) − τwall

Angular momentum L = inertia × angular speed Ω. Coupling Γ transfers it between populations; wall torque removes it. Redistribution changes neutral inertia and speed. Numerical damping is separate from physical heat.

The physics guide motivates each equation and defines its symbols, assumptions and related controls.

Advanced models

Prescribed-field ion orbits use m dv/dt = q(E + v × B). The separate beam-target source uses deuteron stopping, cross sections and tritium loading to estimate neutrons—not neutrinos. Its conceptual blanket is not a complete reactor or net-electricity prediction.

What the computer solves

Inputs pass through a TypeScript worker to plots and Three.js. The centrifuge uses radial finite volumes, Scharfetter–Gummel fluxes and backward-Euler steps; ion orbits use Boris integration. Transport, conductivity and coupling are prescribed. Rendered particles represent populations.

A separate Python / NumPy / SciPy / FiPy backend studies axisymmetric density, momentum, current, energy and species transport. Research replay shows saved collision studies.

What deserves trust?

  1. Conservation, known limits and refinement check implementation and numerical error.
  2. Experimental validation requires matched apparatus, inputs and diagnostics, with held-out predictions and no refitting.
  3. Live qualification records tested browser-model limits and checks that controls, plots and 3D share results. It is a project label, not certification.

The rigid-rotation family fails the historical argon-profile screen under the stated optical interpretation. Lithium predictions remain pedagogical and experimentally unqualified; passing numerical tests does not change that.

What is it useful for?

Use it for learning and reproducible mechanism comparisons. Experiment planning and hardware screening need bounded validation; safety needs separate evidence.
