How Mass Spinner works

Electrical drive → gas rotation → mass separation.

What moves, and why

Current turns ions. Collisions give neutral atoms angular momentum. Radial transport moves particles between radii, so composition and rotational inertia change. Optional thermal evolution calculates temperature changes. The vessel stays still.

The equilibrium reference uses ideal neutral gas at constant temperature and angular speed. During rotation, pressure increases from the axis to the wall. Its gradient gives the inward force for rotation. Heavier atoms then have a larger wall-to-axis density ratio. At constant temperature, faster rotation increases separation. At constant speed, higher temperature decreases separation.

The live model does not calculate radial fluid momentum or extraction.

Torque and heat

Angular momentum L = rotational inertia × angular speed Ω. Γ is the coupling coefficient. Collision torque removes angular momentum from one population and adds it to the other. Wall torque removes angular momentum. Radial redistribution changes neutral inertia and speed. Numerical damping is separate from physical heat.

The physics guide gives the reason for each equation. It gives the symbols, assumptions and related controls.

Advanced models

Ion orbits use prescribed fields: m dv/dt = q(E + v × B). The separate beam-target model calculates neutron production from deuteron stopping, reaction cross sections and tritium loading. These are neutrons, not neutrinos. Its conceptual blanket is not a complete reactor or a net-electricity prediction.

What the computer solves

A TypeScript worker calculates results for the plots and Three.js display. The centrifuge uses radial finite volumes, Scharfetter–Gummel fluxes and backward-Euler steps. Ion orbits use Boris integration. Transport, conductivity and coupling are prescribed inputs. Display particles represent populations, not individual particle paths.

A separate Python / NumPy / SciPy / FiPy backend calculates axisymmetric density, momentum, current, energy and species transport. Research replay shows saved collision studies.

What deserves trust?

  1. Conservation and exact limits give implementation checks. Refinement measures the change when grid cells or timesteps become smaller.
  2. Apparatus-matched inputs and diagnostics are necessary for experimental validation. Prediction tests must use outputs withheld from model development, without refitting.
  3. Live qualification records the tested input range and numerical limits. It includes checks that controls, plots and 3D use the same results. This project term does not mean certification.

Rigid-rotation results are outside the historical argon-profile acceptance limit under the stated optical interpretation. Lithium predictions are learning tools without experimental qualification. Successful numerical tests do not change that limit.

What is it useful for?

Use the simulator for comparisons between mechanisms. Compare results under stated assumptions. Validation within a defined range is necessary for experiment planning and hardware selection. Separate evidence is necessary for safety decisions.
