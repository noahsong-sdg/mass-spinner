What is solved

A fixed deuterium inventory shares one ion/electron temperature (Tᵢ=Tₑ). Prescribed radial compression at fixed length couples density, temperature, pressure work and energy-confinement loss. D–D yields use Bosch–Hale thermal reactivities.

The speed is each plasmoid's center-of-mass-frame speed; relative collision speed is twice that. Translation is a normalized phase animation, not a computed trajectory. Ten seconds of playback spans one pulse. Field curves show an analytic reversed-field topology, not a solved equilibrium.

Dimensions, materials, timing and efficiencies are illustrative. Stability, reconnection, circuit dynamics, fusion heating, fuel depletion and secondary D–T / D–³He burn are unsolved. No Laurelin operating point is calibrated.

Recovered work is an assumed fraction of expansion work. Fusion energy is separate; electrical fusion recovery is unsolved. The plasma driver ledger omits coil stored energy and circuit dynamics. Residual thermal energy is discarded at reset.
