# Independent surface mapping audit

This review traces production solver outputs into the new engineering geometry and quantitative surface contracts. It is an independent audit, not an apparatus calibration or a GPU/browser observation. The managed browser origin denial remains in force.

## Traced physical ownership

- `physics.ts::simulate` evolves SI `[x,y,z]` positions with axial `z` and localized absorbing impacts. Each species closes `injected = wall + end + source + remaining`. Position samples are trajectories, not number density. Impact overlays must retain the original incident denominator; these finite initial populations do not establish a continuous flux or throughput.
- `doublewell-dynamics.ts::simulateDoublewell` exports final species densities in 20 annuli. Their measures are exactly `π(r_outer²-r_inner²)L`; the equal representative weight is declared density times initial support volume divided by the combined initial marker count. The source support is a disk times a finite axial slug, or the entire cylinder for the equilibrium initial condition. Only active particles enter the final density. Its impacts export radius, energy and time, but no full spatial position. A localized wall spot or wall axial profile therefore cannot be reconstructed. The axisymmetric reduction also does not resolve azimuthal fluctuations.
- `centrifuge.ts` exports ion densities relative to total axis ion density. `gaussian-centrifuge.ts` separately exports the prescribed absolute total density. The two closures must not share an invented absolute normalization. Static equilibria predict neither collection nor relaxation time.
- `coupled-target.ts` exports film-only isotope occupancies and film-plus-backing thermal cell averages. Thermal depths are cell centers; the material contact is the face at film thickness, not the midpoint between unequal adjacent cell widths. The source assumes uniform illuminated area, so depth profiles support axisymmetric uniform-area surfaces, not lateral hot spots. Cooling pipes, material appearance, film-contact engineering and structural alloys are illustrative construction assumptions.
- `geometries.ts` gyro loci are actual transverse curves. Curved-guide output is scalar arc length versus cross-field deflection under a guiding-center approximation. Neither output carries absorber acceptance or a solved wall field. Effective radial potential curves are single-particle energy landscapes, not plasma density.

## Original gaps and selected boundary

The preceding target adapter retained only film temperatures despite the backing thermal solver output. In simple terms, a computed hot or cold backing was missing from the 3D connection. A dedicated accepted-result contract is the coherent fix because it can represent the two material regions with their actual independent cell widths and provenance; painting a guessed temperature onto an arbitrary old mesh would not fix the ownership problem.

The Blender meshes use conceptual display dimensions. An explicit SI solver domain and independently labeled illustrative construction are required to connect real surface locations. This keeps true film thickness and collector positions recoverable without claiming that the source paper supplied undisclosed Marathon fabrication drawings. Material colors are identification, not a prediction of alloy temperature, voltage or coil current.

## Adversarial findings addressed during review

1. The first surface contract treated `doublewell` impact `energyJ` as nonnegative kinetic energy. It is the signed laboratory Hamiltonian, including a negative quartic potential. Cold ions can leave a short column with negative total energy. In plain terms, choosing the potential zero cannot make a valid exit disappear. The owner now preserves it as `hamiltonianJ` with no nonnegative check or heat interpretation. Keeping the signed quantity is preferable to clamping or taking its absolute value because those alternatives fabricate deposited heat and change conservation meaning.
2. The first geometry contract registered absorbing collectors for static equilibrium and effective-potential landscapes. The hardware builder shared an overly broad cylinder branch. Simply, a drawn end plate appeared to imply a collection calculation. The owner now distinguishes illustrative boundaries from solved absorbers; closed dynamic runs explicitly require their reflecting/periodic boundary mode. This changes the semantic contract at the actual source, rather than relying on a caption to contradict it.
3. An extra unrecognized impact destination could initially bypass checks that counted only the three known destinations. Exact event-array length plus destination membership now enforces the original incident partition. This rejects inconsistent data rather than renormalizing it into apparently credible recovery.
4. Valid-looking double-well radial curves initially lacked a density-to-population check. The owner now verifies the integral over exact annular volumes against active count times physical representative weight. This is the appropriate invariant; integrating in radius alone omits the cylindrical Jacobian and can manufacture a biased density map.
5. A first draft imported a nonexistent potential helper. Source tracing found it before execution; the owner replaced the import with the same explicit prescribed quadratic potential used by the orbit model. This was a code-contract error, not a newly validated physics approximation.

Curved geometry rejects bend lengths exceeding one circumference and aperture radius reaching the curvature radius. The solver's control domain is broader than this illustrative construction domain. The renderer must expose that unsupported geometry state and keep production plots accessible; silently shortening the bend or substituting a different apparatus would misrepresent the selected inputs.

The original [Alchemy paper](https://www.marathonfusion.com/alchemy.pdf) was reopened during this review. Section 4.1 and Table 1 confirm the 5 mm tungsten, 10/30 mm vanadium-alloy structures and 30 mm Eurofer97 reference construction. The paper specifies asymmetric tokamak blanket dimensions; an illustrative cylindrical shell is not the actual source CAD and must stay labeled accordingly.
