# Reactor views

**Centrifuge** is fuel-processing equipment, not the fusion core. **Reactor** opens Marathon’s current beam reactor; its scene selector also opens the separate fusion-power reference.

- **Beam reactor:** RF ion source → electrostatic acceleration → deuterons striking a cooled lithium-tritide target. Component roles follow [Marathon’s published architecture](https://www.marathonfusion.com/beam-driven-fusion/). Apparatus dimensions and construction alloys are not public; the geometry is illustrative. Existing beam/target calculations remain separate from the power reference.
- **Power reactor · reference:** the six blanket layers retain the dimensions of [the alchemy study, §4.1, Table 1](https://www.marathonfusion.com/alchemy.pdf#page=13): major radius 4.2 m and minor radius 1.2 m. This is a representative tokamak model, not Marathon’s as-built plant.

The added plasma location envelope, toroidal and poloidal magnets, central solenoid, cryostat, supports and heat/fuel interfaces provide conceptual context. Their sizes, count and optical materials are assumptions, not disclosed engineering. The transparent enclosure and removed sector expose the interior; neither describes a physical operating opening. Generic component roles follow [ITER’s machine overview](https://www.iter.org/machine).

Drag to orbit; zoom to inspect; click components or use **Inspect** to read their purpose and material provenance. Blender supplies the actual interactive mesh. No plasma equilibrium, magnet field, shielding, structural qualification, cryogenic system or integrated power-plant performance is solved by these additions. Published neutronics reference values remain frozen.
