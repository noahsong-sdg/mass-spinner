## Paths checked and root causes

### Physical thermal-capacity entry

The Exposure reference creates a log10 capacity range and converts it back with `10**value` before `simulateTargetExposure`. The generic exact editor previously had no scale/unit declaration and interpreted its stored exponent as the physical capacity. Simply, the user edited the dial position rather than the displayed J m⁻² K⁻¹. `src/target-exposure-ui.js` now supplies the established `data-range-scale`/`data-unit` contract consumed by `precise-controls.js`; adjacent Celsius and conductance controls remain linear in their displayed units.

The controls implementation also corrected a related physical-units bug: suffix-matching `endsWith('K')` mistakenly included capacity and conductance names as temperature fields. Explicit `coolantK`/`initialK` membership now limits the 273.15 offset to Celsius temperature controls, so default capacity/conductance are 10000 J m⁻² K⁻¹ and 5000 W m⁻² K⁻¹, giving τ=2 s. In simple terms, units ending in K do not all measure temperature. The thermal equation remains unchanged. Scale metadata and explicit physical-quantity membership are preferable to guessing from captions/suffixes or modifying the thermal solver. `tests/reference-controls-v43.test.ts` checks an actual 5000 off-grid entry through UI/export to physical capacity and thermal time constant, defaults, bounds/reset and adjacent linear/temperature controls. Its actual nested UI test passes as test 671 in the fresh gate.
