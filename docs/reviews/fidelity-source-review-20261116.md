## Smallest meaningful live physics increment

The proposed radial-shear kernel already exists: `fuel-spatial-operation.ts` → current solver, accepted species transfers, `advanceFuelAdvectedMomentum`, mixture thermal/liner solve. It carries accepted mass into local angular momentum, deposits physical shear and signed centrifugal work, and separates BE/upwind losses. Do not duplicate it. The current live `fuel-operation.ts` instead retains two global angular momenta, despite already evolving radial inventories, inertia and optional gas/liner heat.

Expose the existing spatial backend as a **separate optional constrained research experiment**, initially at a declared supported setup, with its own canonical state/export, local Ω plot and numerical status. Its electrical boundary arrays are voltage/contact inputs; they cannot silently inherit the live imposed-current/ion-slip slider semantics. Preserve the same accepted species/momentum/thermal step. Do not present a display-only non-rigid shape on top of rigid dynamics as this integration.

Prior work does not qualify arbitrary browser conditions. The retained v67 review records genuine earliest-common-endpoint surface differences of 4.63712% in time and 2.88464% in space against unchanged 1%/2% local-response targets, plus untested fine-only time support. Later arithmetic-aware conservation passes do not resolve them. Keep those limits visible; qualify only the exact supported observables/time window after bounded actual trajectory/refinement checks. This backend remains z-independent common-fluid rotation with prescribed diagonal electrical transport and overdamped independent-species mobility, not full meridional hydrodynamics.
