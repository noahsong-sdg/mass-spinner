# Text review · 19 November 2026

## Scope

This pass applies [ASD-STE100 Issue 9](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf) to maintained tutorials, reference pages, development documents, and interface text.
It includes controls, diagrams, component descriptions, assumptions, and runtime messages.
The review used the rules and dictionary, including approved meanings and parts of speech.
Sentence length alone does not establish compliance. This is not formal STE certification.

Historical studies, raw results, source titles, quotations, licenses, and embedded GLB provenance keep their original text.
A text review does not qualify a changed binary asset.

## Changes and reasons

| Problem | Cause | Fix and reason |
|---|---|---|
| Explanations introduced equations before their purpose. Terms differed between pages and controls. | Text comes from Markdown, HTML, TypeScript, and display catalogs. A Markdown-only pass cannot reach all visible text. | State the physical question first. Use short instructions and consistent terms across the source paths. Keep the existing publication system. |
| Some scientific wording was too broad. | An equilibrium trend can depend on which quantities stay constant. Density, composition, purity, and recovery are different quantities. | State the conditions and define each quantity. A larger mass gives a larger wall-to-axis equilibrium density ratio, not always a larger absolute density derivative. |
| Phone tutorial actions were delayed. | Each action sent an apparatus-change event. Even an unchanged selection called `workspace.refresh()`, `setActive(true)`, and `showGeometry(latest)`. This rebuilt the same 3D machine. | Send the event only when the requested apparatus is not selected and active. Remove unnecessary scene work instead of increasing assertion timeouts. |
| A selected apparatus can be inactive in Advanced. | The selector keeps its previous value. Its change event also activates Marathon. A selected-value check alone suppresses this necessary event. | Read active level/context through the workspace callback. Keep activation from all three Advanced playgrounds. |
| The combined browser job exceeded its total time limit. | Desktop and phone ran serially, with setup inside the same 25-minute limit. A preceding successful job already took approximately 24 minutes. | Use separate isolated layout jobs. Keep all tests, resources, retries, and timeouts. Publish the same candidate only after both jobs pass. |

The tutorials connect drive, rotation, heating, separation, and collection.
They explain why stronger drive can increase heating, why hotter gas separates less at fixed speed, and why purity alone does not give product quantity.

The pass also keeps these distinctions:

- Prescribed fields exclude plasma feedback on those fields, not particle response.
- Pure feeds keep their composition. The heavy/light separation-factor ratio uses a mixed feed.
- Inventory cuts do not calculate extraction flow.
- Numerical dissipation is not physical heat.
- Equilibrium references are not calculated time histories.
- Numerical verification is not experimental validation.
