## What is already addressed

The current `main.js → worker → centrifuge.ts/physics.ts/geometries.ts → focused-diagnostics.js` path already contains cylindrical inventory and radial cuts, selectable radial resolution/refinement, exact cold rotation branches and wall/exit competition, finite-Pθ double-well landscapes, a view-only radial crop and the reduced-mode frequency fix. Reimplementing these would not address the remaining physics gaps.

The largest distinction is between **more trustworthy predictions inside the existing reduced model** and **a new physical closure**. Both matter; increasing particle count alone will never repair a wrong field/electron/source model.

## Additional consistency gate for the Gaussian alternative

The IIC review derives the Gaussian/current equilibrium under an imposed B and negligible electron inertia. A nonzero azimuthal plasma current also changes B through Ampère's law. Consequently, treating B as externally prescribed is a low-self-field approximation, not a fully self-consistent electromagnetic equilibrium. This is an inference from the proposed equations and Maxwell's magnetostatic relation, not a claimed result of a fitted device.

With cylindrical symmetry, `dBz/dr = −μ0 Jθ`. At minimum, integrate the required Jθ to report an estimated magnetic perturbation relative to the imposed field, and report the neglected electron-inertia force relative to the retained terms. Do not invent a universal instability/validity threshold from those ratios. If perturbations are appreciable, the next model must solve magnetic/current closure rather than relabeling the imposed-field calculation as self-consistent. An absolute N0 is necessary to turn normalized profiles into A/m² and this field estimate.

## What to postpone

Full PIC, turbulence penalties, arbitrary spin-up/relaxation sliders, industrial throughput and power claims are not the next small step. Self-consistent potential formation and experimental comparison need measured field/potential/source/temperature/charge-state/neutral/collector inputs. The 2024 end-electrode study supports this limitation: an electrode command does not uniquely determine the bulk potential. A held-out experimental condition is required before claiming predictive machine validity.
