## Scientific changes and limits

**Collision transport:** the browser-resolution study ran 66/66 full 200 µs cases with six fixed seeds, joint particle/cell/time refinement, collision-off, shifted-lattice and rigid-orientation controls. A lower prescribed electron temperature changes the screening diagnostic environment, not the ion collision law. Terminal-ledger reconciliation excludes timed-out, duplicate or mismatched artifacts; it does not infer success from the existence of a file. All raw budgets remain unchanged.

The result is **not qualification**: 59/60 collisional runs exceed the 1% unresolved-exposure budget; 28/60 exceed the 0.1% raw angular-error budget. There are 275 PASS and 265 UNKNOWN decisions across 540 refinement/sensitivity tests. No FAIL in that table does not convert UNKNOWN into PASS. Current-run observable status recomputes actual diagnostics and always withholds unsupported continuum claims. See [collision qualification](COLLISION-QUALIFICATION-V38.md).

**Material geometry and coating:** `nLi=N/L` is now explicit, with independent inputs or density-linked edits. Invalid linked edits roll back before solver listeners see an inconsistent pair. The stopping-domain map keeps the unresolved stopped-tail boundary. A separate exact constant-flux Li/D/T coating balance reports thickness, mass and sensible-energy ledgers. It is external, not moving-interface regeneration or a thermal/chemical coupling to the fixed reacting film. Output metadata and labels distinguish D–T counts from omitted D–D branches. See [material realism](MATERIAL-REALISM-V38.md).

The finite-film extension completed 16 native RustBCA cases. An independent streaming reducer checked all 5.33 GB of raw output, 128 file hashes, endpoint partitions, projectile-energy accounting, 64 Wilson intervals and eight predeclared sampling decisions. The collapsed reference panel exposes the actual geometry/count/seed. Its broad 18.83-percentage-point sampling budget is not percent-level convergence; the projectile ledger is not a recoil/thermal closure. No uncalibrated recording is silently imported as a live retention kernel. See [native results](FINITE-FILM-V38.md) and [independent audit](FINITE-FILM-INDEPENDENT-V38.md).

Independent review corrected repeated full-occupancy rejection caused by floating-point roundoff and tiny-export cancellation caused by subtracting large inventories. A declared roundoff admission bound leaves raw populations unchanged; analytically integrated positive erosion weights retain small outgoing isotope counts. These fixes preserve ledgers rather than clip or renormalize populations. Decay-network aggregate overflow is rejected at its real finite-number boundary. See [independent realism review](INDEPENDENT-REALISM-V38.md).

**Experimental depth:** passive collector masks use complete impact positions and the original injected denominator. Empty purity is undefined. Research exposes 63 actual archived states, 378 saved distribution marginals and 38 unchanged coarse/fine records; physical entropy qualification remains UNKNOWN. A separate H-1 first-capture tracer localizes events along exact prescribed uniform-B orbits, follows the fast neutral to its absorbing boundary, and retains the reservoir-origin slow-ion inventory and charge. Both products contribute to the angular ledger. This is a restricted straight-through-product approximation with E=RF=0, not generalized separator transport: differential scattering, further secondary atomic kinetics and plasma feedback remain unresolved. It preserves the generic-ion backend rather than applying hydrogen data to abstract 40/84-u ions. See [depth experiments](DEPTH-EXPERIMENTS-V38.md).

Independent H-1 review reproduced a grazing-wall defect: the analytic charged position could round just outside the cylinder, and a ray formula requiring an interior start returned a negative flight time. Simply, floating-point rounding placed a boundary event on the wrong side. The fix admits only a declared `32ε × radius²` arithmetic boundary band as immediate absorption and rejects genuinely exterior positions; strictly interior roots remain unchanged. This enforces the absorbing-boundary invariant instead of blindly clamping every negative root. Twenty independent/producer/kernel/UI tests and strict module checking pass. The display also withholds unresolved many-turn 3D polylines rather than draw an aliased helix; the exact transverse footprint is shown with repeated identical turns omitted. See [independent tracer review](HYDROGEN-TRACER-INDEPENDENT-V38.md).

**Neutrons and inventories:** the first-flight model remains distinct from the fixed-family evaluated-data OpenMC study. The generic positive Bateman solver supports irradiation and cooldown with explicit sources and sinks; its rates are user scenarios, not isotope production forecasts or clinical yields. Data processing, Monte Carlo error and interpolation discrepancies are separate evidence. See [neutron depth](NEUTRON-DEPTH-V38.md).

The original coarse response grid failed at 4 cm: moderation makes absorption strongly curved, so a straight interpolation segment missed the withheld calculation. A separately frozen refinement promotes that sample to training and tests five fresh holdouts under the unchanged tolerances; all ten channel checks pass. Keeping piecewise-linear interpolation with a denser supported grid preserves nonnegative source-normalized responses without an unconstrained high-order fit. The synthetic pure-scatterer also exposed OpenMC's documented `1e-10 cm⁻¹` zero-absorption floor. Its original exact-zero failure remains retained; a separate test checks the actual upstream floor identity without subtracting physical tallies. An NJOY conversion warning was traced to a capture-photon distribution; photons are explicitly not transported in this neutron-only experiment. Neither correction is empirical nuclear-data validation.
